# Analyze and Predict Videogames Sales Numbers

I will use a Kaggle dataset of Videogames from 1980 to 2024 to analyze trends in their sales, as well as train a Linear Regression to predict them. As a lifetime gamer, this project gives me the chance to explore an industry I am into, as well as flex and sharpen my data skills!
